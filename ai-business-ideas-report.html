#1

AI Compliance & HR Toolkit for Small Business Owners

"Know what the law requires — without hiring a lawyer."
Monthly Revenue Potential
$8K – $40K
Target Price Point
$49 – $129/mo
Build Difficulty
Medium

The Problem

New state AI laws hit in 2026 — Colorado's SB 24-205 and Illinois HB 3773 impose fines up to $20,000 per violation. Most small business owners have zero AI governance policy, no written HR AI guidelines, and no idea they're even exposed. They can't afford an employment attorney on retainer.

Target Customer

Small business owners with 2–25 employees in Colorado, Illinois, and any state with pending AI employment law. Especially retail, food service, healthcare clinics, and professional services — anywhere AI touches hiring or performance decisions.

Why It's Underserved

Less than half of all organizations will use AI in HR in 2026 — and almost none have formal governance in place. Legal firms don't serve this affordably. Existing HR software ignores compliance prompting entirely. The regulatory window is wide open: the businesses that get fined first will create a wave of demand.

What You Build

A prompt toolkit + plain-English checklist system that walks a small business owner through: writing a compliant AI use policy, auditing vendor AI tools, building an employee notification template for AI-assisted decisions, and generating a quarterly compliance review. No legal jargon. Subscription with state-specific update alerts.

#2

AI Operations Toolkit for Home Service & Trade Businesses

"For plumbers, electricians, and HVAC techs — who hate paperwork."
Monthly Revenue Potential
$6K – $30K
Target Price Point
$19 – $49/mo
Build Difficulty
Low

The Problem

Only 25% of residential contractors have meaningfully integrated AI into their workflows as of early 2026 — yet among those who have, 38% report measurable business impact. The other 75% are drowning in quote follow-ups, no-show management, seasonal scheduling surges, and review requests they never send.

Target Customer

Solo and small-crew trade businesses: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, pressure washing, and lawn care. Owner-operators aged 35–55 who are skeptical of tech but motivated by saving time and winning more jobs.

Why It's Underserved

Existing AI tools are built for office workers. Trade businesses need prompts written in plain language for real-world scenarios: "Write me a text to follow up on an unanswered quote," "Draft a Google review request for a happy HVAC customer," "Write a seasonal surge auto-reply for my voicemail." No tool is nailing this vertical. Entry barrier is low; loyalty is high once they see it work.

What You Build

A done-for-you prompt library organized by job phase: Lead follow-up, Quote delivery, Scheduling, Post-job review requests, Upsell scripts, and Off-season reactivation. Delivered as a web app or Notion/Google Doc subscription. Upsell: industry-specific add-on packs (e.g., HVAC seasonal pack, roofing storm response pack).

#3

AI Customer Service Script Toolkit for Local Retailers & Restaurants

"Handle complaints, requests, and reviews without losing your cool."
Monthly Revenue Potential
$5K – $25K
Target Price Point
$29 – $79/mo
Build Difficulty
Low

The Problem

Customer service is the #2 use case for AI in small business — but most local retailers and restaurants are still writing every response manually. A 1-star review sits unanswered for days. A refund request goes sideways. A complaint email triggers an emotional reply. Each one damages reputation or costs a repeat customer.

Target Customer

Brick-and-mortar local businesses: independent restaurants, boutique retail, salons, gyms, and service businesses with a customer-facing reputation. Owners who manage their own Google, Yelp, and social DMs — and dread every notification.

Why It's Underserved

Tools like Intercom and Tidio serve mid-market. Nothing serves the owner of a single pizza shop who gets 3 Yelp reviews a week and needs to respond well — without paying $200/month for enterprise chat software. The gap is in done-for-you language templates that are brand-voice-flexible, legally safe, and emotionally intelligent. It's a volume play: tens of thousands of these businesses exist in every metro area.

What You Build

A fill-in-the-blank prompt system covering: 1-star review responses, refund and dispute language, loyalty reactivation messages, apology scripts, and "we're short-staffed" communications. Organized by scenario. Delivered as a lightweight web tool or Notion database. Upsell: a custom brand-voice calibration session.

#4

AI Sales Outreach Toolkit for B2B Service Providers

"Stop staring at a blank email. Start closing."
Monthly Revenue Potential
$10K – $50K
Target Price Point
$49 – $149/mo
Build Difficulty
Medium

The Problem

Small B2B service providers — accountants, IT consultants, marketing freelancers, staffing agencies — win business through outreach but hate writing it. They know their service cold but freeze when writing a cold email, a LinkedIn DM, a proposal intro, or a follow-up after a no-response. Their pipeline leaks from weak copy, not weak offerings.

Target Customer

Solo consultants and small agencies (1–10 people) in B2B professional services: bookkeepers, IT support shops, marketing agencies, HR consultants, and insurance brokers. Revenue range $100K–$1M/year. They've heard of AI but haven't committed to learning it.

Why It's Underserved

Sales AI tools (Apollo, Instantly, Clay) are built for growth-stage companies with SDR teams and $1K+/month budgets. Small B2B providers have neither the budget nor the technical appetite for those platforms. A prompt toolkit priced at $49–$99/month that outputs a ready-to-send cold email, a follow-up sequence, and a proposal template in 10 minutes is a fundamentally different product — and the market gap is wide.

What You Build

Prompt templates by sales stage: ICP identification, cold outreach by channel (email, LinkedIn, phone script), proposal narrative, objection response scripts, and closed-lost reactivation. Includes a "pain point library" by industry vertical. Upsell: a done-for-you 30-day outreach sequence for their specific service.

#5

AI Pricing & Revenue Strategy Toolkit for Product-Based Small Businesses

"Charge what you're worth. Finally."
Monthly Revenue Potential
$4K – $20K
Target Price Point
$39 – $99/mo
Build Difficulty
Medium

The Problem

Small product-based businesses — Etsy sellers, boutique retailers, food producers, print shops — consistently underprice out of fear and guesswork. They have no pricing framework, no competitive analysis process, and no language for communicating premium value to customers. AI is now being used in pricing by enterprise retailers, but zero tools exist to bring this down to the small business level.

Target Customer

Product-based small business owners: handmade goods sellers, specialty food brands, independent boutiques, print-on-demand shops, and local makers selling online and in-person. Typically doing $20K–$300K/year in revenue and leaving margin on the table every quarter.

Why It's Underserved

PwC and the US Chamber of Commerce both flagged pricing as an "emerging AI frontier" in 2026 — but every tool in this space is enterprise-grade and data-intensive. A prompt toolkit that walks a candle maker through competitive price benchmarking, value-based pricing language for their product descriptions, discount strategy scripts, and seasonal pricing calendars doesn't exist at a small business price point. First-mover advantage is real here.

What You Build

A prompt-guided pricing workbook covering: cost-plus vs. value-based pricing analysis, competitor research prompts, product description language that justifies premium pricing, discount and bundle strategy templates, and a quarterly pricing review checklist. Delivered as a web tool or Notion system. Upsell: a 1-on-1 pricing audit session powered by your own AI workflow.
